    Local information.
    Honest about its limits.

    Vet After Hours is an independent directory for Victoria, Greater Sydney and South East Queensland (Brisbane, Gold Coast and Sunshine Coast). Clinic information comes from published websites. It is not a live availability service or veterinary advice.

    Victoria is included where a clinic website describes an after-hours, on-call or 24-hour service, including regional towns. Greater Sydney listings are website-checked 24-hour hospitals and published after-hours services. South East Queensland listings cover Brisbane, the Gold Coast and the Sunshine Coast. Some towns are not listed, and other Australian states are not covered yet. Confirm species acceptance with the clinic. Distances use suburb centres and are approximate straight-line estimates, not driving distances.
