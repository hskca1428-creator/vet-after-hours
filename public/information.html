Privacy & data sources

Updated 28 November 2026

Your searches and location

Suburb search and distance calculations run in your browser. This version has no accounts, advertising trackers or newsletter signup. If you choose “Use my location”, your browser asks permission. Coordinates are used for the current search and are not stored by this app or sent to an app server.

Hosting providers may process standard request information, such as IP addresses, to serve and protect the website. Clinic website and Google Maps links open third-party services with their own privacy practices. Directions links contain the clinic destination, not your browser coordinates.

Reporting a correction

“Report an issue” opens your email app with a draft addressed to hello@vetafterhours.au. Nothing is sent automatically. If you send it, your email address and message will be handled through our email service to investigate the correction. Please avoid sending medical records or other sensitive information. Contact us to request deletion of your correspondence.

Clinic information

Each clinic card links to its published source and shows the date its website information was reviewed. A website review is not direct clinic confirmation. We do not know whether a clinic can currently accept patients. Call before travelling. The directory lists clinics in Victoria, Greater Sydney (NSW) and South East Queensland (Brisbane, Gold Coast and Sunshine Coast) whose websites describe after-hours or 24-hour care. Victorian regional towns and Greater Sydney clinics were checked on 26 November 2026. South East Queensland clinics were checked on 28 November 2026. Other states are not listed yet. It is not a complete list of every clinic, and it is not veterinary advice.

Locality data and distance estimates

Victorian locality names, postcodes and approximate centres are derived from joelkoen/postcodes-au, retrieved 15 September 2026. The Victorian file keeps names, postcodes, state and coordinates. Clinic coordinates use their suburb centres, not verified street locations. Distances are approximate straight-line estimates and should not be interpreted as driving distances or travel times.

Greater Sydney locality names, postcodes and approximate centres, including Central Coast, Blue Mountains and Hawkesbury localities in the extract, come from Matthew Proctor’s Australian Postcodes, retrieved 26 November 2026. That list is credited as CC0. Some postcode groups share a coarse centroid. These are locality points, not surveyed clinic entrances.

South East Queensland locality names, postcodes and approximate centres, covering Greater Brisbane (including Logan, Ipswich, Moreton Bay and Redland), the Gold Coast and the Sunshine Coast, come from Matthew Proctor’s Australian Postcodes, retrieved 28 November 2026. That list is credited as CC0. Some postcode groups share a coarse centroid. These are locality points, not surveyed clinic entrances.
