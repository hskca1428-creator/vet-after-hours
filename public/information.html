Privacy & data sources

Preview version · 15 September 2026

Your searches and location

Suburb search and distance calculations run in your browser. This version has no accounts, advertising trackers or newsletter signup. If you choose “Use my location”, your browser asks permission. Coordinates are used for the current search and are not stored by this app or sent to an app server.

Hosting providers may process standard request information, such as IP addresses, to serve and protect the website. Clinic website and Google Maps links open third-party services with their own privacy practices. Directions links contain the clinic destination, not your browser coordinates.

Reporting a correction

“Report an issue” opens your email app with a draft addressed to hello@vetafterhours.au. Nothing is sent automatically. If you send it, your email address and message will be handled through our email service to investigate the correction. Please avoid sending medical records or other sensitive information. Contact us to request deletion of your correspondence.

Clinic information

Each clinic card links to its published source and shows the date its website information was reviewed. A website review is not direct clinic confirmation. We do not know whether a clinic can currently accept patients. Call before travelling. This pilot has incomplete regional coverage and is not veterinary advice.

Locality data and distance estimates

Victorian locality names, postcodes and approximate centres are derived from joelkoen/postcodes-au, retrieved 15 September 2026. The app filters the source to Victoria and retains names, postcodes and coordinates. Clinic coordinates use their suburb centres, not verified street locations. Distances are approximate straight-line estimates and should not be interpreted as driving distances or travel times.
